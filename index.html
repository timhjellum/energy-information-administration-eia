The Challenge

EIA's public website had grown to more than a million pages over more than a decade — built on aging JavaScript, non-responsive layouts, and inconsistent markup. Every new initiative meant working around code that predated modern web standards entirely. I was directed to convert the legacy site to responsive, mobile-friendly pages, modernizing the JavaScript to HTML5 and ECMAScript 2015 standards along the way, without disrupting a site the public depended on for energy data.
